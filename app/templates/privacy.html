Privacy notice

This page explains what happens to the information you give this site, in plain language. It describes what the BanTheFake software does as it is published.

The petition

When you sign, we store:

  • your name,
  • your email address (trimmed and lowercased, so each address can sign only once),
  • your location, if you entered one (it is optional),
  • the date and time you signed (in UTC).

That is all. We do not store your IP address with your signature, and we do not set cookies.

We use these details to count signatures and to show lawmakers and platforms who supports the campaign. The public page only shows the total number of signatures, never names or emails. Site administrators can download the list of signatures. We never share your email.

Signatures are kept until they are deleted. The software does not delete them automatically.

Deleting your signature

Contact us at {{CONTACT_LINK}} and ask us to delete your signature. Give the email address you signed with (if you write by email, send it from that address). An administrator removes it from the database, and it is no longer counted or exported.

The media scanner

  • Your file is sent to our server only so it can be scanned. It is written to a temporary file during the scan and deleted as soon as the scan finishes, including when the scan fails.
  • We do not store or log your file, its contents, or its file name.
  • The analysis, including the AI detector model, runs on our server. Your file is not sent to any other company or API.
  • The error level analysis heatmap is made in your browser and is not uploaded.

The letter generator

The letter is built in your browser. The name and town you type into it are never sent to our server.

IP addresses and logs

To stop abuse, the server limits how many scans, petition requests and failed attempts to access administrator tools can come from one IP address. It keeps full recent IP addresses in memory only, for up to about an hour after your last request covered by a limit (the longest limit window is one hour; expired addresses are removed within a minute after that), and then discards them. Full IP addresses are never written to disk or to the database, and they are cleared when the server restarts.

Failed attempts to access administrator tools are logged as security warnings, but the software shortens IPv4 addresses to a /24 network and IPv6 addresses to a /48 network before logging them. It never logs the supplied administrator token. The shortened network may be retained in server logs according to the site operator's logging policy.

The standard BanTheFake server setup turns off web access logs, so visits are not logged by the app. If this site runs behind a hosting provider or proxy, that service may keep its own logs. Contact us to ask what applies to this site.

Third parties

  • Google Fonts. Our pages load fonts from Google (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com). Your browser connects to Google to download them, so Google receives your IP address and browser details, as with any web request. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Links to other services. The takedown and support services we link to (such as StopNCII.org and Take It Down) are run by other organizations under their own privacy policies.

We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking scripts.
